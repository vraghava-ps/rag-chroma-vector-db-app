Cognizant Technology Solutions is a global technology services and consulting company that helps businesses build artificial intelligence (AI) solutions, modernize IT systems, and automate operations.

The company provides DevOps, Cloud, Java, Python, React, Angular and Generative AI development services.

The company working hours are Monday to Friday from 9AM to 7PM IST.

Employees receive 20 paid leaves every year.

The company provides health insurance to all full-time employees.

The notice period for employees is 90 days.

The company has offices in Hyderabad, Bangalore, Kolkata, Chennai, Pune, Mysore, Mangalore, Coimbatore, Delhi and Noida.

The company headquaters is in Chennai.

Ravi Kumar Singisetti has been the CEO of the company since January 2023, replacing Brian Humphries.

Cognizant has approximately 356,700 employees worldwide.

 More than 250,000 employees are based in India across major hubs like Chennai, Hyderabad, and Bengaluru.
